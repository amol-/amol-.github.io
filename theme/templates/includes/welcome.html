Welcome

A bit about me

I'm a Software Engineer and father of two, based in Torino, Italy.

I've been leading software development teams for more than 20 years and, surprisingly, Software Development remains my main passion.

Work and experience

I currently work as a Principal Software Engineer at Posit, where I help create impactful projects for the Python ecosystem.

Previously, I was Senior Director of Open Source Engineering at Voltron Data, where I coordinated contributions to and maintenance of core data-engineering and data-analytics projects such as Apache Arrow, Ibis, and Substrait.

Over the years, I've also founded the Python company AXANT.it, become a shareholder in Mamazen Startup Studio, and been a founding member of the IH1 investment holding.

I've also advised several startups and invested in some of them as an angel investor, most recently in the Commodore Reboot Initiative.

Python and Opensource

Python is also a big part of what I do outside of work. I've contributed to some major Python projects you may have used or heard of, including PyArrow and TurboGears.

I've written Python-related books, spoken at conferences around Europe, appeared as a guest on several online shows about Software Engineering, and maintain several OpenSource projects.

I also mix my interests in data engineering and Python in the Monthly Python Data Engineering newsletter.
